---
name: Public channel destinations
description: User-confirmed authorization and product boundary for moving existing public channels.
---

The channel owner may move a public channel only into a public community that they also own. Do not offer someone else's public community, a private community, a paid business workspace, or the global public network as a destination without asking for a change in scope.

Keep the free plan limited to one public community per owner. Additional public communities are separate from paid business workspaces. New access uses recurring external payment confirmed by an administrator, who records a paid-through date and can end access. Pending requests never activate access. Previously approved permanent slots remain valid; do not silently revoke purchases that were sold under the earlier terms.

**Why:** The user selected owner-controlled public communities rather than unrestricted public communities or category-only moves. They later clarified that new access is recurring, with external payment and administrator renewal or termination instead of a connected payment processor. Moving a channel carries its existing members and messages, so broadening destinations changes exposure and ownership boundaries.

**How to apply:** For future channel organization changes, enforce destination ownership, public status, and active subscription for subscriber communities on the server at mutation time, not only in UI options. Preserve channel identity and history, and clear category associations that belong to the previous space.
