UPGRADE MY EXISTING IRC INTO A BUSINESS AI AUTOMATION PLATFORM.

IMPORTANT:

The existing IRC is NOT being converted into a public social/community platform.

The IRC is the CORE BUSINESS COMMUNICATION AND OPERATIONS SYSTEM for an AI automation company.

Keep the existing IRC architecture and real-time messaging functionality, but repurpose and expand it so businesses can use private IRC-style workspaces to manage leads, customers, employees, contractors, AI agents, jobs, appointments, and business communications.

DO NOT rebuild the application from scratch.

FIRST inspect the existing codebase and understand:

* Current frontend
* Current backend
* Database
* Authentication
* Users
* Channels
* Messaging
* Real-time communication
* Permissions
* API routes
* Existing settings

Extend the current system wherever possible.

==================================================
BUSINESS TENANCY

This must be a multi-business SaaS.

Each business is completely isolated.

Business A must NEVER be able to access:

* Business B users
* Business B channels
* Business B messages
* Business B customers
* Business B leads
* Business B jobs
* Business B AI agents
* Business B settings

Enforce this on the backend.

Never rely on frontend restrictions.

IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT RULE

Do NOT turn this into a generic Discord clone.

Do NOT remove the IRC identity.

Do NOT rebuild the existing application from scratch.

The IRC is the FOUNDATION of the business platform.

The goal is:

IRC + CRM + AI + Jobs + Appointments + Automation

The IRC is where employees, contractors, and AI agents communicate and where business events are surfaced.

Build the system incrementally.

First inspect the existing code.

Then implement:

PHASE 1
Business/workspace architecture

PHASE 2
Business permissions

PHASE 3
Business channels

PHASE 4
Leads and customers

PHASE 5
AI agents

PHASE 6
Appointments

PHASE 7
Jobs

PHASE 8
Automations

PHASE 9
External communication

PHASE 10
Business dashboard

PHASE 11
SaaS infrastructure

After every phase, test existing IRC functionality and make sure no existing functionality is broken.

The finished product should be a business communication and automation platform built on top of the existing IRC.
